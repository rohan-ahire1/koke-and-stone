# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Two audiences, in this order:
1. People reviewing Rohan Ahire's frontend work who open this site from his portfolio. They judge whether he can build a site that feels crafted and alive.
2. Inside the fiction: homeowners and small businesses who want a moss or Japanese-style garden, a courtyard or a terrarium, and are deciding whether to book a garden visit.

## Product Purpose
A portfolio demo website for "Koke & Stone", a fictional moss-garden design studio, built by Rohan Ahire as a frontend work sample. Success: a visitor finishes it convinced he builds premium, living websites; within the fiction, the page reads as a real, bookable garden studio.

## Positioning
Koke & Stone (koke, 苔, is Japanese for moss) designs, plants and tends moss gardens, courtyards and terrariums, then stays on for seasonal care. The site's proof of craft is its tokonoma alcove: one moss arrangement on a dark ledge that changes with the season, a hanging scroll that unrolls with the process, and gardens framed like temple windows.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Koke & Stone is fictional. The site must say so plainly (an "A concept by Rohan Ahire" link in the side rail, pinned on phones, the gardens intro, a tag on every project and the footer credit) so no visitor mistakes it for a real business.
- Contact uses a non-working example address (hello@kokeandstone.example). No prices, no booking system.

## Brand Commitments
- Tokonoma alcove world (chosen 2026-10-20, replacing the dark green depth-effect design): pale plaster, weathered pine, a dark lacquered ledge, sumi ink. Green appears only in the moss itself; one iris blue marks actions and the lit season.
- Motion is limited to two authored moments: the season row cross-fading the alcove, and the process scroll unrolling, plus a slight lean of the garden photos under the pointer. All of it switches off under reduced motion.

## Evidence on Hand
- All imagery is AI-generated (originals kept outside the repo).
- Projects shown are fictional demo gardens and must be labelled as concept work; no real client names, testimonials or stats.

## Product Principles
- Every effect should feel like the garden itself, never decoration on top of it.
- Calm over spectacle: the page should feel slow and cared-for.
- Honest framing: the fiction is always disclosed.
